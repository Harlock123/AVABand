# Credits: art

The pictures AVABand shows at moments of note. All are made for AVABand (CC0), rendered by the tools named below. Your own pictures of the same names, in `<AppData>/AVABand/art/`, are shown instead.

## unique.jpg, stairs-down.jpg, stairs-up.jpg, stairs-up-town.jpg, level-cavern.jpg, level-labyrinth.jpg, level-fortress.jpg, level-moria.jpg, level-lair.jpg, level-gauntlet.jpg, danger.jpg, death.jpg

- **The stairs, the kinds of level, a deadly level, and death** — made for AVABand, rendered by `tools/scene_art_levels.py` (no source images; the same renderer as the Word of Recall's), so every scene matches: stairs going down into the dark and up toward grey light or the evening sky over the town; a rough cave with pools; a high labyrinth corridor with ways off it; a fortress wall with a red-lit gate; the dwarves' pillared halls; a lair of bones with eyes in the dark; a trapped corridor with jets of fire; a hall lit red, full of eyes; a headstone at dusk; a cloaked figure in a lit doorway, its eyes shining. These replace the public-domain paintings the scenes showed before.

## deep-descent.jpg, trapdoor.jpg, quest-complete.jpg, boss-slain.jpg

- **Falls, a quest done, a great foe slain** — made for AVABand, rendered by `tools/scene_art.py` (no source images; it reuses the Word of Recall renderer): a shaft falling away to a red glow with the floor's rubble; a trap door fallen open over a dark chute; a sealed letter of thanks on the Prancing Pony's table; a fallen crown and a broken blade in a shaft of light. The game animates them (`Controls/SceneView.cs`).
- Licence: CC0 1.0 (public-domain dedication).
