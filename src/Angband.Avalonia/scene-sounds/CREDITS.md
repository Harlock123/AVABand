# Credits: scene sounds

The sounds of AVABand's scenes, synthesised for AVABand by `tools/scene_sounds.py` from noise and
oscillators (no samples). CC0 1.0 (public-domain dedication).

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `recall-reach.ogg` | Word of Recall: the rent tearing open, then a rising, shimmering rush as the hand reaches |
| `recall-take.ogg` | Word of Recall: the hand closing, and a thunderclap as its light takes you |
| `stairs-down.ogg` | Footsteps going down stone stairs, fainter and more echoing with each |
| `stairs-up.ogg` | Footsteps coming up, nearer each, into open air |
| `death.ogg` | A single deep funeral bell |
| `unique.ogg` | A swelling low chord at a first meeting with a unique |
| `danger.ogg` | A low rumble under a dissonant drone, for a deadly level |

Your own files of the same names (`.ogg`, `.wav` or `.mp3`) in `<AppData>/AVABand/scene-sounds/`
are played instead.
