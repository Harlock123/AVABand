# Birth options

Chosen when you make a character and fixed for its life (Settings → Options shows them).

- **Generate a new, random artifact set** — artifacts are made fresh for this character.
- **Generate connected stairs** — arrive on a staircase leading back.
- **Force player descent** — stairs only go down, and Word of Recall one level deeper.
- **Word of Recall has no effect** — until Morgoth is dead.
- **Restrict creation of artifacts** — no artifacts at all.
- **Stack objects on the floor** — off, a square holds one object.
- **Lose artifacts when leaving level** — an artifact you leave behind is gone for good.
- **Show level feelings** — the arrival messages about danger and treasure.
- **Increase gold drops but disable selling** — shops pay nothing, the dungeon more (the default).
  Game → *Shops pay gold when you sell* changes your mind mid-game; it isn't a cheat, and the
  character still enters the high scores. A burnt-out torch sells for nothing, whichever you choose.
- **Start with a kit of useful gear** — your class's kit, as Angband 4.2.5 gives it, paid for out of
  600 gold; off, you start with one food and one light and keep the gold the rest would have cost.
- **Monsters learn from their mistakes** — they stop using attacks you resist.
- **Know all runes on birth** / **Know all flavors on birth** — skip the learning.
- **AVABand's quests** — the Prancing Pony, its notice board, and the quests below (on by default).
- **The strongroom** — lockers at the Prancing Pony shared by all your characters (on by default).
- **AVABand's racial abilities** — each race's extra ability (Help → Your character); off, the races
  are Angband 4.2.5's as they are (on by default).
- **Angband 4.2's blows** — blows from your weapon's weight, Strength and Dexterity, heavy weapons
  hard to wield, and casters' armour weight costing mana, as in Angband 4.2.5 (on by default); off,
  each class strikes a fixed number of times.
- **AVABand's encumbrance** — Strength keeps adding to what you carry where Angband's stops,
  Constitution adds a little, worn gear weighs three quarters, Heroism and Berserk help, and the
  burden is named (Help → Your character); off, Angband 4.2.5's rule (on by default).
- **AVABand's easier casting** — spells fail half as often as in Angband 4.2.5: the least a class
  can reach drops from 5% to 2%, and fear's penalty is halved too, but stunning and amnesia still
  count in full (Help → Magic). On by default, and on for characters made before it existed.
- **Ghosts of fallen characters** — now and then, on a new level at the depth one of your dead
  characters fell at, its ghost walks; beat it to lay it to rest and win back one of the things it
  wore (Help → Monsters). On by default; never in the daily dungeon or the tutorial.
- **Hirelings** — a sword for hire at the Prancing Pony follows you down for a fee and a share of
  your gold (Help → Quests); never in the daily dungeon or the tutorial (on by default).
- **Persistent levels (experimental)** — levels are kept when you leave them.
- **To-damage is a percentage of dice (experimental)** — to-dam makes each damage die bigger by a
  percentage instead of adding to every blow; slays and brands multiply the dice; criticals add
  dice; everyone has at least two blows.

The **cheat** options (Settings → Options) show you what monsters and levels were made, or let you
survive death; using one keeps the character out of the high scores. So does any **Debug** menu
command — but its tick *Character marked by debug (not scored)* takes that mark off again (say, for
a save marked by the old debug "Shops pay gold" switch). Ticking it puts the mark back. It can't
clear a cheat option.
