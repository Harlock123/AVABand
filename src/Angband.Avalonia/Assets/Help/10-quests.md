# Quests

Besides Angband's own quests (Sauron on level 99, Morgoth on level 100), AVABand has quests of
its own — on by default; the birth option *AVABand's quests* turns them off for a new character.
Each has a **thing** to find and a **place** to use it, and some a choice to make. Your journal is
the **Quests** page of the Knowledge screen (`~`).

## The Prancing Pony

The inn in town (the `9`). Walk in and Butterbur will tell you who has work: the quests on offer
depend on your level. There is also a **notice board** with smaller jobs — hunt so many of a
monster, or bring back so many of something found only in the dungeon. You can have two board jobs
at a time; the postings change whenever you come back up to town, and you collect your pay at the
inn.

## The quests

- **The Sealed Door** (from level 5): Durgash the Keybearer holds a dwarven key in his hall. Take it
  from him, and walk into the sealed door deeper down to open it — there is treasure behind it.
- **The Burden** (found, not offered): somewhere below 400 ft lies the Seal of Angmar. Wear it and
  you are faster and stronger — but it won't come off, and it calls every evil thing near, worn or
  carried. Only a dwarven forge further down can unmake it: step onto the forge.
- **The Broken Blade** (from level 10): three shards of a great sword lie at three depths, each
  guarded. Bring them to the Weapon Smiths (2), and choose two powers for the reforged blade from
  the slays, brands and resistances whose runes you have learned.
- **Consecration** (from level 15): Hathol, Lord of the Barrow, cannot die while his altar lies
  profaned — strike him down and he rises whole. Step onto his altar and pour out the Water of Ulmo
  you were given, and then he can die.
- **The Letter** (from level 8): take a sealed letter to the hermit below the town (step onto his
  door). You were asked not to read it. You can, though — and then deliver it anyway, show it to the
  Alchemist (5), or burn it. The shops remember what you did.
- **The Thief of the Black Market** (from level 9): the Black Market's strongbox was stolen by one
  of four suspects. Pages of its ledger, carried by monsters above 1000 ft, each hold a clue; put
  them together, and hunt the one they point to — only the thief has the box. Bring it back to the
  Black market (6).

Quest items can't be dropped, sold, thrown, ignored or stolen; one carried by a monster goes into
your pack when it dies (or, if your pack is full, falls beside it — leave it behind and Butterbur
will have it for you at the inn). A quest's place turns up on the level it names; if you leave before
you're done, it's there again next time you arrive.
