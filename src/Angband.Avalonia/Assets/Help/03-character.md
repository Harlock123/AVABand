# Your character

## Race and class

Your **race** (Human, Half-Elf, Elf, Hobbit, Gnome, Dwarf, Half-Orc, Half-Troll, Dúnadan,
High-Elf, Kobold) shifts your stats and skills and gives some abilities — Dwarves sense ore,
Hobbits know mushrooms, High-Elves see invisible things.

Your **class** decides how you fight and what magic you have:

- **Warrior** — the best fighter, no magic.
- **Mage** — arcane spells from books; frail but powerful.
- **Priest** — divine prayers, granted a random one from the book you study.
- **Ranger** — a fine archer with some nature magic.
- **Druid** — nature magic and shape-changing; fights without weapons.
- **Rogue** — stealthy, a thief (`s` steals), with some arcane magic.
- **Paladin** — a holy fighter with prayers.
- **Necromancer** — casts from the dark: sees without light, fails more in it.
- **Blackguard** — a brutal fighter whose spells feed on combat.

## Stats at birth

The creation screen offers four ways to set your stats:

- **Point-buy** (Angband's): 20 points to spend, stats 10 to 18; unspent points become gold.
- **Rolled** (Angband's): each stat 8 to 17 by dice; *Reroll* for another set.
- **Heroic roll** (AVABand's): each stat 14 to 18/50 — superlative characters are common.
- **Heroic point-buy** (AVABand's): 50 points, stats up to 18/50.

With a rolled method, set a **Min** for any stat (the final value, after race and class) and
press **Autoroll**: it rerolls, thousands of times if need be, until every minimum is met, or
tells you a minimum is out of reach. Heroic characters still enter the high scores, marked
*[Heroic]*, but their score counts only three quarters: heroic stats make the game easier.

## Stats

- **Strength** — melee damage, how much you carry, digging.
- **Intelligence** — arcane casters' spells and mana.
- **Wisdom** — divine casters' spells and mana, and saving throws.
- **Dexterity** — blows, to-hit, armour, avoiding thieves.
- **Constitution** — hit points.

Stats above 18 are written 18/10, 18/50 and so on up to 18/220. Drained stats come back when you
gain a level, and with certain mushrooms and potions (Vigor, Restoration).

## Experience and levels

Killing monsters, learning runes and casting new spells give experience. Each level brings hit
points, better skills and, for casters, more spells. Some monsters and traps drain experience;
it returns when you regain it, or with a Potion of Restore Life Levels.

## The character sheet

`C` shows everything: your title for your level, age, height and weight, the background you were
born with, your race's and class's abilities, stats, skills, resistances, and a paper doll of what
you wear. **Save to
file** writes it to a text file, as Angband's character dumps. At the end is your **history** — when
you set out, each level reached, each unique killed, each artifact found — and `:` adds a note of
your own to it. An artifact gone for good (left on a level, or sold off by a shop) is marked
**(LOST)**, and one you never found but lost anyway reads "Missed the ...".
