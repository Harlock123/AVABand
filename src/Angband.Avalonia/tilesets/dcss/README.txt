A selection of the Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup 32x32 tiles, released as CC0
(public domain) by their artists. See LICENSE.txt.
https://opengameart.org/content/dungeon-crawl-32x32-tiles
Courtesy links: http://code.google.com/p/crawl-tiles/ and http://rltiles.sourceforge.net/
Files keep their original names and folders.

avaband_web.png (the spider web) was drawn for AVABand, as the pack has none;
it is released under CC0 (public domain) like the rest. So are avaband_shovel.png,
avaband_pick.png, avaband_mattock.png and the three avaband_*bolt.png crossbow bolts,
drawn by tools/avaband_drawn_tiles.py; the "tinted/" pictures are recoloured copies of
the pack's own, made by tools/dcss_flavor_tiles.py so that no two flavours look alike.
