Credits:
  - DawnBringer: the 16-colour palette every sprite is drawn in.
  - DragonDePlatino: all the artwork.

    (https://github.com/tommyettinger/DawnLikeAtlas, CC BY 4.0), used to match
    sprites to AVABand's monsters, objects and terrain.

Changes: dawnlike.png holds only the cells AVABand uses, cut from DawnLike's
sheets (the first animation frame of each creature) and repacked 16 to a row;
the pixels are unchanged. tileset.json (the mapping of game ids to tiles) was
generated by tools/dawnlike_tileset.py.

DragonDePlatino's request - "Inside "Reptiles.png" you will find a Platino sprite. If you use
Dawnlike, you MUST use this sprite and hide him very well!" - is honoured as his README suggests:
one in a thousand of AVABand's woodchucks (blue yeeks) is drawn as Platino (the monster-rare:
tile in tileset.json).

ava_*.png (the sealed door, dwarven forge, barrow altar, hermit's door and the Key of Belegost, for
AVABand's quests) were drawn for AVABand by tools/avaband_quest_tiles.py and are CC0 (public domain).
