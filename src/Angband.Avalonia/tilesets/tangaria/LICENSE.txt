Tangaria's tileset (32x32), from Tangaria, the online multiplayer roguelike powered by PWMAngband.
https://tangaria.com/tileset/
Files taken from https://github.com/igroglaz/Tangaria_release (lib/tiles/tangaria/: 32x32.png,
graf-tan.prf, flvr-tan.prf; lib/gamedata/flavor.txt).

The tileset also includes tiles ported from Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup, whose developers and artists
published them under CC0; Tangaria thanks them on the page above.

Changes: tangaria.png holds only the tiles AVABand uses, cropped from Tangaria's 4096x4096 32x32.png
and packed into one sheet (converted to 8 bits per channel). The artwork itself is not altered.

tileset.json (the mapping of AVABand's game ids to tiles) was generated from Tangaria's graf-tan.prf
and flvr-tan.prf by tools/tangaria_tileset.py (monsters Tangaria lacks or names differently are drawn
with the nearest Tangaria creature), then given per-glyph fallbacks by tools/tileset_glyph_fallbacks.py.

ava_*.png (the sealed door, dwarven forge, barrow altar, hermit's door and the Key of Belegost, for
AVABand's quests) were drawn for AVABand by tools/avaband_quest_tiles.py and are CC0 (public domain).
