tileset.json was generated from Angband's graf-*.prf, xtra-*.prf and flvr-*.prf by
tools/angband_prf_to_tileset.py (rebuilt by tools/angband_extra_tilesets.sh), with fallbacks per
monster letter from tools/tileset_glyph_fallbacks.py. Rubble and the block-fall trap borrow their
neighbours' tiles.

web.png and tree.png were made for AVABand and are CC0 (public domain): the set has no spider
web (this is the Adam Bolt folder's web, scaled down) and no tree creature (tree.png stands for
huorns, ents and Old Man Willow).
