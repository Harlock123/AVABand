What was modified
-----------------
- The teal (#476c6c) background of the source BMPs is transparent in creature,
  object and trap tiles, and replaced by the NetHack room floor in terrain tiles.
- Many flavours (potions, rings, amulets, wands, staves, rods, mushrooms) use
  recoloured copies of a pack image, so that each flavour has its own colour.
- The tiles used are packed into one sheet, rltiles.png.

Creatures, dungeon features and traps come from the NetHack tiles (nh-mon0,
nh-mon1, nh-dngn); objects from the shared item folder; a few gaps (block walls,
hydras, bears, beetles, griffons, frogs...) from the Dungeon Crawl tiles. No
picture that is also in the "dcss" tileset is used.

tileset.json and rltiles.png were generated by tools/rltiles_tileset.py
(with monster-glyph fallbacks added by tools/tileset_glyph_fallbacks.py).
